Placement Description

For anyone who has a sweet-tooth, or even just a chance craving, Trish’s Chocolate has everything chocolate you could ever desire. This includes chocolate-dipped strawberries, fudge, chocolate turtles, truffles and even caramel apples. Plus too many other guilty pleasures to list!

They can be assigned as chocolate dippers, chocolate makers,  caramel apple dippers and cash register handlers as the primary job. The secondary work would be cleaning, which may include sweeping, mopping, washing dishes and throwing the trash during open hours and after closing the store. These assignments would be rotating with all the other employees of the store.
